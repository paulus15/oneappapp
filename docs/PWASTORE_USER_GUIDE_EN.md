# PWA Store user guide

> The English version of the interface is still being translated. Where a screen is only available in French for now, the French label is given in brackets.

## What is PWA Store?

PWA Store is a store for web apps. Every app is presented by a verified publisher and reviewed by our team before it goes live. You can open apps, install them on your device, keep them as favourites and share your opinion.

## What is a PWA?

A PWA (Progressive Web App) is an app that runs in your web browser, without going through a traditional app store. Many PWAs can also be **installed**: they then get an icon on your home screen, open in their own window and, for some of them, work offline.

## Create an account

1. Choose **Sign up** and enter your name, email address, country and a password.
2. You receive a **6-digit code** by email. Enter it to confirm your address. The code is valid for ten minutes.
3. No code? Check your spam folder, then use “Resend code”.

You can browse the Store without an account. An account lets you keep favourites and a library, and post reviews.

For extra security, turn on **two-step verification** under “My space” → “Account security” (« Mon espace » → « Sécurité du compte »): a code will be emailed to you each time you sign in.

## Open and install an app

1. Open the app’s page.
2. Choose **Launch the app** or **Open to install** (« Lancer l’application », « Ouvrir pour installer »): the app opens in a new tab.
3. To install it, use your browser’s offer (an “Install” button or menu item, or “Add to Home Screen”). On iPhone and iPad, use Safari’s Share button, then “Add to Home Screen”.

Installation depends on your browser and device: PWA Store cannot force it. Once installed, you can choose “Mark this PWA as installed” (« Déclarer cette PWA installée ») to find it in your library.

## Badges

| Badge                       | Meaning                                                                                                                                                    |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Hosted by PWA Store**     | The app’s files were uploaded to PWA Store, analysed and approved, and are served by PWA Store from an address of their own, kept separate from the Store. |
| **Hosted by the publisher** | The app runs on its publisher’s website. PWA Store checked its address and listing, but its content can change without a new analysis.                     |
| Category, languages         | The kind of app and the languages it is available in.                                                                                                      |

Hover over or tap a hosting badge to read its explanation.

## Favourites

On an app’s page, choose **Add to favourites** (heart icon). Your favourites are listed in “My space”. Tap the button again to remove the app.

## Reviews

- To rate an app, you need an account with a confirmed email address.
- One review per app (up to 2,000 characters). You can edit or delete it from “My space” → “My reviews”.
- The publisher can reply. Reviews that break the rules may be hidden by our team.

## Report a problem

On an app’s page, or under a review, choose **Report** and pick a reason (dangerous content, scam, privacy breach, broken app, impersonation…). Our team looks at every report. You can follow the outcome in “My space”.

## Contact us

Use the **Contact** page (link at the bottom of every page): enter your name, email, subject and message. The **Help** page answers common questions.

## Choose your language

The language selector (**FR / EN**) is in the navigation bar. Your choice is remembered on this device. The English translation is **in progress**: some pages, as well as emails, are still in French for now.

## Delete your account

Under “My space” → “Account security”, choose **Deactivate my account** and confirm with your password and the code sent by email. Your account is deactivated straight away; your personal data is **anonymised 30 days later**. Your reviews stay visible, but anonymous.

Before that, you can download your data from “My space” → “Preferences and data”.
